## Local image-search runtime

The JavaScript image-search runtime additionally uses Transformers.js 4.3.1 (Apache-2.0), ONNX Runtime 1.30.0 (MIT), and sharp 0.35.5 (Apache-2.0). The native sharp packages include libvips and its image-codec dependencies; their supplied license notices remain with the packaged binaries.
